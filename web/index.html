One voice, four octaves, about 60 notes fit in one SMS. Start from a MIDI file or play it in yourself. Everything happens in your browser, your files aren't uploaded anywhere.

1. Convert a MIDI

Ringtones are monophonic: one track is picked as the melody and where notes overlap the highest one wins. Converting replaces the tune in the editor (Undo brings it back).

2. Listen and change it

Click a key to add a note after the selected one. Click a note in the roll to select it, drag it up or down to change the pitch. Your computer keyboard plays too (the keys are labelled on the piano), P is a rest, . dots, ← → move, Backspace deletes, Space plays.
